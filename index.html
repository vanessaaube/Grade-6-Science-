Explain with particle theory

Use complete sentences and scientific vocabulary. These responses are saved in this browser but are not part of the auto-score.

Compare with a strong answer

When heat energy is added, particles gain energy and move faster. The spaces between them usually increase, and the attractive forces between them weaken. Enough added energy can cause a change from solid to liquid or liquid to gas.

Compare with a strong answer

As heat energy is removed, water particles lose energy and move more slowly. They become arranged in a solid crystalline structure. Water is unusual because this frozen structure takes up more space, making ice less dense than liquid water.

Compare with a strong answer

Construction materials expand when heated and contract when cooled. Different materials may change size at different rates, creating stress and cracks. Expansion joints, insulation, and suitable materials give structures room to move safely.

Compare with a strong answer

Water expands when it freezes, so ice is less dense than liquid water and floats. The surface ice forms an insulating layer that helps keep deeper water liquid, allowing aquatic organisms to survive through winter.
